Next steps

Open Claude Multi Desktop from Applications and choose Add an account, or in Terminal run:

claude-multi-desktop add Work
claude-multi-desktop open Work

Then sign in with your other account. Guide: https://jackson-cmd.github.io/claude-multi-desktop/

To uninstall: sudo rm /usr/local/bin/claude-multi-desktop, delete Claude Multi Desktop from Applications, and run sudo pkgutil --forget io.github.jackson-cmd.claude-multi-desktop.
